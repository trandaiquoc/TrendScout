1. Demonstration service

TrendScout helps users inspect candidate keywords and their source evidence. The hosted Vercel dashboard keeps SQLite and legacy run writes read-only. A separate Shared LIVE Fetch can run only for sources enabled by the operator and when the shared Firestore quota policy is valid. The current demo can select Wikimedia Pageviews, Google Trends/Search, Bing Search, and YouTube Search. Selecting Bing also runs the Google candidate step first; a fresh shared Google cache avoids another Google provider request. Publishing these static policy pages does not itself enable a provider or Fetch feature.

The demonstration may be unavailable, incomplete, changed, or discontinued. It has no user accounts and may use synthetic fixtures in offline mode.

2. Permitted use

Use TrendScout lawfully and for evaluation. Do not bypass source approvals, consent checks, rate limits, retention controls, or other access protections. Do not disrupt a provider or service, or use source queries to collect personal information.

3. Source evidence and limitations

TrendScout displays provider-reported records, collection times, query scope, and application-generated summaries. Search-result position is not search volume, popularity, purchase intent, or proof of a trend. A US region or language filter does not prove that creators, authors, viewers, or readers are located in the United States.

Keyword candidates and any future indicators depend on the selected provider, its coverage, the sample, observation window, and the application's methods. They are exploratory evidence only. TrendScout does not guarantee accuracy, availability, future demand, or US market demand; do not rely on it as the sole basis for business, purchasing, or investment decisions.

YouTube records are currently excluded from keyword extraction, trend scoring, and product recommendations.
