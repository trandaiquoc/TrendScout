1. Demonstration service

TrendScout is a local demonstration for exploring candidate keywords and reviewing their source evidence. It may use synthetic fixtures in offline mode and may contact selected data providers only when an operator configures and enables the corresponding source. Features and data coverage can change as the demonstration is developed.

These pages may be published as static policy documents. Their publication does not publish or operate the local backend.

2. Permitted use

Use the demonstration lawfully and for evaluation. Do not attempt to bypass its source approvals, consent checks, rate limits, retention controls, or other access protections. Do not use it to disrupt a provider or service, or to collect personal information through source queries.

The current demonstration has no user accounts and no self-service account controls. You are responsible for keeping access to your local computer and browser storage appropriate for your use.

3. Source evidence and limitations

TrendScout presents provider-reported records, collection times, query scope, and application-generated summaries. Search position is not search volume, view count, popularity, purchase intent, or proof of a trend. A US region filter does not prove that creators or viewers are located in the United States.

Keyword candidates and any future indicators depend on the selected provider, its coverage, the sample, the observation window, and the application's methods. They are exploratory evidence, not a guarantee of accuracy, future demand, availability, or commercial outcome. TrendScout currently excludes YouTube records from keyword extraction and scoring.

It does not remove data held by YouTube, browser local storage on your device, or copies in backups or console output outside the application.
